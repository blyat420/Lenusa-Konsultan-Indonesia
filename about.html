ABOUT US

PT Lenusa Konsultan Indonesia provides specialized consulting, professional training, and software solutions in geotechnical and hydrogeological engineering. We support the mining industry through technical expertise and practical solutions tailored to operational requirements.

Our Background

PT Lenusa Konsultan Indonesia operates across three key areas of the mining industry: geotechnical and hydrogeological consulting, professional training, and specialized software solutions. Through these areas, we provide technical expertise, develop professional competencies, and support the application of engineering technologies to address industry requirements. Our work focuses on delivering practical knowledge and technical solutions that contribute to more effective and reliable mining operations.

Vision & Mission

Our vision and mission guide our commitment to delivering reliable technical services, developing professional capabilities, and supporting the evolving needs of the mining industry.

Our Vision

To become a trusted provider of geotechnical and hydrogeological consulting, professional training, and specialized software solutions within the mining industry. Through technical expertise, continuous improvement, and technological advancement, we strive to support safer, more efficient, and sustainable mining operations while creating long-term value for our clients and industry partners.

Our Mission

  • Deliver tailored, innovative solutions to address the unique challenges of mining operations.
  • Enhance mining productivity and safety through expert consulting, training, and project services.
  • Foster long-term partnerships by exceeding client expectations with professionalism and excellence.
  • Commit to sustainability by integrating environmental considerations into every solution we offer.
  • Empower the mining workforce with world-class training programs designed to elevate technical skills and expertise.
