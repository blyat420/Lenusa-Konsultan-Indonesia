Our Official Partner

  • Waterloo Hydrogeologic

    Waterloo Hydrogeologic develops specialized software solutions for groundwater professionals and water resource management. Its software portfolio supports groundwater modeling, aquifer analysis, contaminant transport assessment, and hydrogeological data interpretation to assist professionals in evaluating and managing groundwater resources.

  • Seequent

    Seequent, a Bentley Systems company, develops geoscience software solutions for analyzing and interpreting complex geological and geotechnical data. Its software supports applications across mining, civil engineering, environmental management, geothermal energy, and groundwater, helping professionals gain insights from technical data and support engineering decisions.
